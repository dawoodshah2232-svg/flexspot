How much RAM does a laptop need in 2026? The honest answer

8GB vs 16GB vs 32GB explained: how much laptop RAM you actually need for office work, development, and creative workloads in 2026.

Published 2026-09-25. The full article is below the fold when JavaScript runs — the headlines and key facts above are readable without it.

> Key takeaways: > - 16GB is the 2026 sweet spot for most buyers — it's now the base config on machines like the MacBook Air 15 M4. > - 8GB is workable for light use only; heavy multitaskers will feel it. > - 24–32GB+ is for developers, video editors, and local-AI users — not a status symbol. > - On Apple Silicon and most thin laptops, RAM is soldered — buy what you'll need in 3 years, not today.

The short answer

In 2026, buy 16GB unless you have a specific reason to buy more or you know your workload is genuinely light. RAM is the one spec you can't upgrade later on most modern laptops, so err on the side of headroom.
