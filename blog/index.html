Questions, answered straight.

What is FlexSpot?

FlexSpot is a worldwide product specs and comparison database — look up full specifications for phones, laptops, GPUs, tablets, wearables and audio, and compare any two products side by side before you buy.

Is FlexSpot free?

Yes. Every spec sheet, comparison and guide is free, with no account required.

Where do FlexSpot specs come from?

From manufacturers' official spec sheets and reputable tech publications. Every product page lists its sources, and unknown specs are left blank rather than guessed.

Can I compare two phones on FlexSpot?

Yes — the Compare page lines up any two products row by row: display, performance, camera, battery, price and more, with differences highlighted.

Does FlexSpot sell the products it lists?

No. Retailer links take you to the manufacturer or retailer site; you buy directly from them. FlexSpot takes no cut and sells nothing.
