Every platform has an early-adopter story. The first hundred users of anything — a social app, a marketplace, a launch platform — get something the ten-thousandth user never will: a founding position, visible proof they were there first, and the compounding advantage of being early on a board that's only going to get more crowded.

On SpotFlex, that early-adopter layer is formalized as the **Founding 100**: spots numbered 1 through 100, seeded as the platform's founding set. This guide explains what founding spots are, what's still available, and exactly how to claim one.

## What a founding spot is

A founding spot is a regular SpotFlex spot — a public profile page for your brand, startup, project, or community that can be boosted, ranked, compared, and shared like any other — with one permanent difference: it carries a **founding number between 1 and 100**.

That number is part of the spot's identity. It shows on the spot's profile with a founder badge, and it marks the spot as one of the first hundred ever seeded on the platform. The platform's own data model treats founding spots as a distinct set (founding numbers 1–100), and spot profiles with a founding number display a "Founding spot #N" banner.

Think of it like a low license plate number or an early Twitter handle: the underlying asset works the same, but the number signals seniority. On a leaderboard-driven platform, where attention is the currency, being visibly early is a real asset — people notice #7 in a way they don't notice #4,318.

## What's claimed and what's still open

The founding set includes brands across categories — the platform seeded 100 real external brands as founding spots, plus SpotFlex's own project at #1. The key detail for early adopters: **most founding spots are still unclaimed**.

An unclaimed founding spot is a reserved number waiting for its brand. The spot exists on the board with a placeholder identity; when the actual brand claims it, the spot becomes theirs — their logo, their pitch, their link — and the founding number stays with it permanently.

Unclaimed founding spots can be **claimed FREE**. That's not a discount code or a limited promo; it's how the founding program works. The platform wants its founding numbers in the hands of real brands, so the barrier to claiming one is deliberately near zero.

## How claiming a founding spot works

The claim flow is the same one every SpotFlex spot uses, and it's designed to be finished in under a minute:

1. **Find the spot.** Browse the board or open the spot's profile. Unclaimed founding spots show a "Founding spot #N — unclaimed" banner with a "Claim this spot FREE" button.
2. **Start the claim.** The button takes you to the claim page with the spot pre-selected (a link like `/claim?claim=spot-slug`).
3. **Enter your details.** No account is needed — you provide the spot's information: brand name, tagline, description, and website link.
4. **Complete the step.** Standard claims include choosing a boost (starting at $1), but unclaimed founding spots are claimed free — the founding claim is about taking ownership of the number, not about the opening bid.

After claiming, the spot is yours. You can update its details later by contacting the team at support@flexspot.lol. Your founding number doesn't change — #14 stays #14 forever, no matter how many spots launch after you.

A practical note: the platform is currently in Phase 1, where payments and verifications are handled manually by the team (usually within a few hours). For a free founding claim, the process is ownership verification rather than payment — but give the team a little time to process it.

## What early adopters actually get

Let's be concrete about what "founding" means in practice, based on what the platform actually does today:

- **A permanent founding number.** Spots are identified by slug, but founding spots carry their 1–100 number as a visible badge. As the platform grows and thousands of spots launch, the founding hundred remain visually distinct.
- **The founder badge.** Founding spots display founder branding on their profile — a signal to every visitor that this brand was early.
- **Early Adopter recognition.** The platform's rewards system includes an "Early Adopter" distinction for spots among the first 100 claimed — one of the first hundred spots ever claimed gets recognized as such. Claiming a founding spot puts you in that cohort by definition.
- **First-mover board position.** This is the underrated one. Right now, the board is young and ranks are cheap — a modest boost goes a long way. Early adopters can establish high leaderboard positions and referral momentum before the competition thickens. A #3 rank earned in month one is worth far more than the same rank fought over in year two.
- **The referral engine, from day one.** Every spot gets a personal referral link (flexspot.lol/s/your-brand?ref=YOUR-CODE) that you create with just your name — no signup. Share it anywhere, earn 20% commission on payments from people who join through your link, and climb the Top Referrers board. Founding spots that start distributing early build the referral networks that pay out for years.

What's *not* promised: nothing here guarantees traffic, revenue, or rank. Founding status is a positioning advantage, not a results guarantee. The spots that win on SpotFlex will still be the ones that boost smartly, pitch well, and distribute relentlessly. Founding just means you start that race from a better lane.

## Why the platform does this

It's worth understanding the logic, because it tells you how to think about your own launch. New platforms have a cold-start problem: nobody wants to join an empty board, and a board full of placeholders looks dead. The founding program solves it from both sides — the platform seeds real brands as founding spots, and it makes claiming them free so real owners take over quickly.

For the platform, free founding claims are customer acquisition. For you, they're an arbitrage: a numbered, badged, permanently early position on a platform that's explicitly trying to fill its founding roster. That window closes when the hundred are claimed. It doesn't reopen — you can't retroactively become #37.

## A checklist before you claim

If you're considering a founding spot, run through this:

- **Is the brand real?** Founding spots are meant for real brands, projects, and communities. Claim the spot that matches what you actually run.
- **Is your pitch ready?** Your spot profile is a public page. Have a sharp tagline, a clear one-paragraph description, and the right link before you claim — first impressions on a leaderboard stick.
- **Do you have a logo?** Spots with real brand visuals dramatically outperform placeholders. The platform hosts real brand logos for founding spots; make sure yours is represented properly.
- **What's your boost plan?** The claim may be free, but the board is a competition. Decide your opening move: which rank are you aiming for in week one, and what will it take to get there?
- **Who shares your link?** Set up your referral link on day one and give it to your team, your community, your group chats. Early distribution compounds.

## The bottom line

The Founding 100 is a simple proposition: the first hundred spots on SpotFlex are numbered, badged, and — while unclaimed — free to claim. Once they're gone, they're gone, and every brand that joins later competes without the founding marker.

If you run something worth discovering — a startup, a store, a creator brand, a community — check whether your founding spot is still unclaimed. Being early is the one advantage you can never buy later.
