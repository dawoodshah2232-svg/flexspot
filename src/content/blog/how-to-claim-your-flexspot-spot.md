> **Key takeaways:**
> - Claiming takes under a minute: enter your details, pick a starting boost (from $1), and complete payment — no account needed.
> - Your spot goes live after payment verification; prepare your title, description, and link beforehand so it launches strong.
> - The details you enter at claim time become your public profile — write them like a first impression, not a form.
> - After claiming, your real work starts: sharing your spot and referral link is what moves you up the leaderboard.

## What claiming means

Claiming a spot is how your brand enters the FlexSpot leaderboard — the public competition for homepage visibility where spots rank by total verified boosts. Your spot gets a shareable profile page, a personal referral link, and a position on the live leaderboard. For the full concept, read [getting started with FlexSpot](/blog/getting-started-with-flexspot) and [how bid-for-attention marketing works](/blog/what-is-bid-for-attention-marketing).

This post is the practical walkthrough: exactly what happens, screen by screen, and how to do it well.

## Before you start: prepare these

Have these ready before you open the claim flow — deciding under time pressure produces weak profiles:

1. **Your display name.** Your brand, project, or personal name as you want the public to see it.
2. **A one-line description.** What you do, in plain words. (See [how to write a spot profile that gets clicks](/blog/how-to-write-a-spot-profile-that-gets-clicks) — this line does heavy lifting.)
3. **Your link.** The URL visitors go to when they click your spot — your website, store, social profile, or campaign page. Double-check it works.
4. **An image or logo** if the flow asks for one — a clear, recognizable visual.
5. **Your boost amount.** Starting from $1. Decide beforehand: the minimum gets you listed; a larger opening boost buys initial rank.

## Step 1: Open the claim flow

Hit the claim button on the homepage (or any "Start From $1" call to action). The flow opens as a guided modal — you stay on the page, and it walks you through each step. No account creation, no email verification walls, no onboarding quiz.

## Step 2: Enter your spot details

Fill in your display name, description, link, and visual. Write the description for strangers: most leaderboard visitors have never heard of you, so "we help freelance designers get paid faster" beats "synergistic fintech solutions." This is your first impression on every visitor — treat it like one.

**Common mistake:** rushing this step and planning to "fix it later." Your spot's early visitors form their impression from what you enter now. Get it right the first time.

## Step 3: Choose your boost

Your boost is your opening bid in the visibility competition — it determines your starting rank and counts 100% toward your leaderboard total. Key facts:

- **Minimum is $1.** That gets you a live spot on the board.
- **Higher opens rank higher.** [How boosting moves you up](/blog/how-boosting-moves-you-up-the-leaderboard) explains the mechanics — every dollar of verified boost moves you up, and passing someone takes their rank live.
- **You can add more later.** Anyone (including you) can contribute additional boosts to your spot afterward. Your opening boost is a starting position, not a final one.

Strategy note: there's no universally "right" opening amount. A modest open with a strong sharing plan often beats a big open with no promotion — because [referral-driven boosts](/blog/referral-marketing) and community contributions can carry you further than your wallet alone.

## Step 4: Complete payment

After confirming your details and boost, you complete payment. We're currently in Phase 1: payment is completed manually and verified by the team, usually within a few hours. You'll receive confirmation once verified. (Automatic payment processing is coming — the FAQ has the latest status.)

**Payment tips:**
- Double-check your spot details *before* paying — corrections afterward require contacting support.
- Keep your payment confirmation; it speeds up verification if anything needs checking.
- If you're paying with USDT, confirm you're sending on the correct network (TRC-20, BEP-20, or Solana) — see our [USDT advertising guide](/blog/can-you-pay-for-advertising-with-usdt).

## Step 5: You're live — now promote

Once verified, your spot is live on the leaderboard with its shareable profile page and your personal referral link. **This is where most of the game happens.** Claiming gets you on the board; promotion moves you up it:

1. **Share your profile page** everywhere your audience is — social, email, WhatsApp, communities.
2. **Create your referral link** (just your name, no signup) and share it — every visit through it adds $1 to your total, once per visitor per day.
3. **Ask your network** to visit, share, and contribute boosts. Early momentum matters — spots that move fast attract attention, which attracts more movement.
4. **Watch the board.** Leaderboard dynamics are public and real-time — use them. A rival passing you is a reason to rally your audience, not a reason to despair.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Claiming before your link works.** Test your destination URL. A live spot pointing at a broken page wastes your launch moment.
- **A vague description.** "Check out my awesome project!!!" tells visitors nothing. Say what it is and why it matters.
- **Setting and forgetting.** The leaderboard rewards activity. Spots whose owners promote daily outperform spots with bigger opening boosts and silent owners.
- **Ignoring your referral link.** It's the highest-leverage tool you get at claim time — a personal affiliate link paying 20% instant commission on every payment from members who join through it. Not using it is leaving money on the table.
- **Boosting without a sharing plan.** Paid boosts plus promotion compound; paid boosts alone are just a number.

## What happens next

Your spot lives on the board as long as the competition runs. You can boost it further anytime, rally your audience around rank battles, and climb the [Top Referrers board](/blog/referral-marketing) by driving visits. And when you're ready to convert that visibility into customers, make sure the page your spot links to is ready — our [conversion guide](/blog/how-to-turn-website-traffic-into-customers) shows how.

Claiming takes a minute. What you do after claiming is the whole game. Good luck — see you on the board.

## Your first 48 hours: the launch checklist

- [ ] Share your profile page on every channel you have (social, email, WhatsApp, communities).
- [ ] Create your personal referral link and send it to your 10 most supportive contacts individually.
- [ ] Post about your spot with a clear ask: visit, share, boost — tell people exactly how to help.
- [ ] Check your rank and note who's around you — rivals are future motivation.
- [ ] Fix anything you rushed: re-read your description as a stranger, test your link again.
- [ ] Thank early visitors and boosters publicly — recognition fuels more support.

Spots that launch with 48 hours of active promotion establish momentum that's hard to catch. Spots that launch silently start every day from zero. The difference isn't budget — it's the first two days of hustle.

## If something goes wrong

Payment not confirmed after several hours? Details entered incorrectly? Don't panic and don't claim a second spot — contact support with your payment confirmation and the display name you used. Most issues resolve quickly when the team can match your payment to your claim. And if your spot goes live but isn't ranking where you expected, remember: rank reflects total verified boosts, and the leaderboard rewards sustained promotion over one-time spending. The guide to [how boosting moves you up](/blog/how-boosting-moves-you-up-the-leaderboard) explains exactly how rank changes when boosts land.
