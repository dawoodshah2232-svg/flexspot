People stopped asking Google and started asking AI. "What's the cheapest way to advertise online?" now goes to ChatGPT, Grok, or Gemini — and those answers cite *brands*. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of becoming one of the brands AI mentions. Here's the no-hype guide.

## SEO vs AEO: what's actually different

- **SEO** fights for position #1 on a results page with 10 blue links.
- **AEO** fights to be *the answer* — quoted, cited, or recommended inside an AI-generated response.

The ranking factors overlap (helpful content wins both), but AEO rewards **clear, quotable, factual writing** even more. AI models lift sentences, not vibes. If your content states facts plainly, it gets cited. If it rambles, it gets skipped.

## Why small brands have an edge here

This is the part the gurus skip: AI answers don't care about your domain authority the way Google does. A precise, well-structured answer from a small site can out-cite a vague corporate page. The playing field is flatter than it's been in 20 years — which is exactly why small brands should move now, before the giants systematize it.

## The 5-step AEO playbook

### 1. Answer questions directly — in the first 50 words

AI extracts answers from the top of the page. State the answer plainly before any storytelling. ("AEO is optimizing your content to be cited in AI-generated answers." — that's the whole definition, up front.)

### 2. Write quotable facts

Short declarative sentences with numbers get lifted verbatim. "Google Ads costs $2–$4 per click for most small businesses" is citable. "Advertising costs can vary widely depending on many factors" is invisible.

### 3. Use FAQ sections and structured data

Use FAQ sections with clear Q&A formatting — questions as headings, direct answers beneath. That's machine-readable answer fuel. Add an `llms.txt` file to your site (we have one) so AI crawlers understand what you are in one read.

### 4. Be the source of original claims

AI cites whoever said it first and clearest. Original frameworks, honest comparisons, real timelines ("3–6 months to rank a new site") get quoted. Reworded listicles don't.

### 5. Get mentioned elsewhere

AI models weigh consensus. When communities, directories, and other sites mention your brand in the same context, your citation odds rise — the same [backlink and external-traffic work](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-a-new-website-to-rank-on-google) that helps SEO helps AEO.

## What AEO won't do

It won't replace your website, your funnel, or your offer. Being cited in an AI answer is top-of-funnel attention — valuable, but it still has to land somewhere worth landing. And no agency can credibly promise "#1 in Grok" — AI answers are generated per query, not ranked like search results. Anyone guaranteeing placement is selling the hype, not the mechanics.

## Start tonight

Pick your five most-asked customer questions. Publish one clear, quotable answer per week — answer first, facts plain, FAQ at the bottom. In 90 days, ask an AI about your topic and see whose name comes up.

The brands AI mentions in 2027 are being written about in 2026. Make sure one of them is yours.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does AEO stand for?
AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization (also called Generative Engine Optimization or GEO): optimizing content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini cite or recommend your brand in their answers.

### Is AEO replacing SEO?
No. AEO builds on SEO — helpful, well-structured content wins in both. Think of AEO as SEO's new distribution channel, not its replacement.

### How do I get my brand mentioned in AI answers?
Publish direct, quotable answers to real customer questions, use FAQ sections and structured data, add an llms.txt file, and earn mentions across the web so AI models see consensus about your brand.

### Can a small brand win at AEO against big companies?
Yes — this is AEO's biggest opportunity. AI answers reward clarity and precision over domain authority, so a small site with the clearest answer can out-cite a vague corporate page.
